In Focus

Scan a prompt to see how likely it is to lead an AI code generator toward hallucinated output.

Runs entirely in your browser — no prompt text is sent anywhere or stored. Flags are heuristic, not a correctness guarantee: a clean scan doesn't mean the generated code will be correct, and a flagged prompt can still produce good output. Treat it as a second pair of eyes before you hit send.
